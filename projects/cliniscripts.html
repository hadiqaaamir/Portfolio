Context

Healthcare products carry more than interface complexity.

Clinical software must support different roles, changing information and consequential decisions without adding unnecessary cognitive load. The design challenge is not simply arranging features—it is creating workflows that help people understand what needs attention, what the system is doing and what should happen next.

How I work

From an ambiguous requirement to a testable product experience.

01

Frame

Clarify the user, workflow, constraints and intended outcome with clinical, business and technical stakeholders.

02

Map

Translate requirements into journeys, task flows, information hierarchy and the states the experience must support.

03

Prototype

Move from wireframes to high-fidelity interactions, using rapid and AI-assisted prototyping to test ideas in context.

04

Ship and refine

Support implementation, review product quality and use stakeholder feedback and behavioural signals to guide improvements.
