CliniScripts · Module 01B

AI scheduling · Clinic operations · Decision support

Turning scheduling constraints into recommendations people can understand.

I designed a matching workflow that brings client needs, provider availability and clinic rules together while keeping the coordinator in control of the final assignment.

My role

Product design and workflow definition

Users

Schedulers and clinic administrators

Design focus

Constraints, reasoning and oversight

Platform

Part of CliniScripts

01 · Problem

A free calendar slot does not automatically make someone the right clinician.

Scheduling depends on more than time. Client needs, credentials, specialty, location, continuity and clinic policies all affect the decision. The interface needed to reduce manual comparison without hiding why a match was recommended.

01Many constraints

Bring operational and clinical requirements into one flow.

02Explainability

Show the reasons behind a recommendation instead of only a score.

03Human oversight

Let staff compare, adjust and confirm the final assignment.

02 · Process

I translated matching logic into a decision-support experience.

01

Gather constraints

Document the information staff already consider when arranging care.

02

Prioritize inputs

Separate required criteria from preferences and clinic-level rules.

03

Design comparison

Make match reasons, conflicts and alternatives easy to scan.

04

Test and refine

Prototype the flow, review implementation and capture follow-up needs in the backlog.

Decision model

From request to confirmed assignment, with reasoning kept visible.

The interface supports the operational decision without presenting automation as authority.

Care request→Required constraints→Ranked recommendations→Coordinator confirms
