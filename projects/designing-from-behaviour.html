01 · Problem

Analytics reveal patterns. They do not explain them on their own.

A drop, repeated click or underused feature can point to a problem, but the number is only the beginning. I connect behavioural signals with the intended workflow, interface context and stakeholder knowledge to understand what may be happening—and which questions deserve attention first.

02 · Process

A practical loop from signal to design response.

01

Observe

Review clicks, paths, activation and adoption signals to locate unexpected patterns or points of hesitation.

02

Interpret

Place the signal inside the user flow and combine it with stakeholder feedback, requirements and known constraints.

03

Prioritize

Frame a focused hypothesis based on user impact, business relevance and the effort needed to learn more.

04

Design and validate

Refine the experience, support implementation and monitor the same behavioural area after release.

Reading the signals

Different behaviours help answer different product questions.

Click behaviour

Is the interface inviting the expected action?

Repeated, missed or unexpected clicks can expose unclear hierarchy, affordance or navigation.

Activation

Where does early value become difficult to reach?

Entry and setup patterns help focus attention on the steps between arrival and a meaningful first outcome.

Feature adoption

Are useful capabilities being discovered and understood?

Usage patterns can prompt questions about discoverability, relevance, timing and supporting guidance.

Feedback

What context is missing from the data?

Stakeholder and user-facing teams add operational detail that makes behavioural signals more actionable.

Hypothesis loop

Turning an observed pattern into something the team can test.

Signal

Notice a pattern

A step receives attention but does not consistently lead forward.

Question

Locate the uncertainty

Is the next action unclear, poorly timed or disconnected from user intent?

Response

Refine the experience

Adjust hierarchy, language, sequence or feedback to make the path easier to understand.

Follow-up

Monitor and learn

Review the same signals after release and combine them with qualitative feedback.

03 · Execution & learning

Connecting product evidence to everyday design work.

  • Defined questions for analytics review
  • Examined click, activation and adoption patterns
  • Mapped signals back to user journeys
  • Combined quantitative and stakeholder inputs
  • Framed design hypotheses and priorities
  • Refined flows, hierarchy and interface states
  • Supported implementation and product QA
  • Planned follow-up observation after release

Outcome

A clearer, repeatable way to decide what deserves design attention—and why.

This approach makes product conversations more specific, connects interface decisions to observed behaviour and creates a repeatable feedback loop between design, implementation and the next round of observation.
